Dear Valued Property Owner, Resident, and Commercial Client:
SUBJECT: Official Policy on Client Privacy, Property Records, and Data Safeguards

At TankWise Septic Services, we recognize that inviting a professional sanitation crew onto your residential property or commercial compound involves an important trust. We are committed to conducting all mechanized septic tank pumping, CCTV pipeline diagnostics, and scheduled inspections with complete discretion and rigorous data privacy protections.

This formal letter sets forth the principles under which TankWise collects, uses, and safeguards information obtained during online bookings, telephone dispatch, and onsite technical evaluations.

1. Information We Collect for Operational Purposes

When you arrange an assessment, pump-out, or emergency backup callout, we collect only the essential details needed to safely dispatch our vehicles and personnel:

  • Contact Information: Your full name, telephone number, WhatsApp contact, and email address for dispatch confirmations and written service reports.
  • Property & Access Details: Site address, GPS coordinates, gate widths, driveway slope, parking standoff distance, and hose routing requirements.
  • System Specifications: Estimated septic tank capacity in liters, approximate year of construction, previous service dates, and documented plumbing symptoms.

2. Onsite Diagnostic Records & Video Imagery

During camera pipe inspections or drone access surveys, our equipment records high-definition digital footage. We formally confirm that:

  • Camera footage is recorded exclusively to identify structural pipe faults, root ingress, and joint separations in your underground sanitary plumbing.
  • No footage of private household interiors or living quarters is ever recorded.
  • All diagnostic video files and photographs belong solely to you as the property owner and will never be published or shared with third parties without your explicit written authorization.

3. Strict Non-Disclosure to External Parties

TankWise does not sell, lease, monetize, or trade client personal information. We do not provide your telephone number or service records to marketing aggregators, real estate brokers, or external telemarketers. Information is shared only with certified disposal facilities as strictly required by Tamil Nadu Pollution Control Board (TNPCB) compliance manifests.

4. Secure Retention of Service Health Logs

For clients enrolled in our Routine Maintenance Plans, we maintain a secure historical service ledger (sludge depth measurements, baffle check notes, and pump intervals). This data is archived in encrypted digital systems to help you prove historical maintenance for municipal compliance, property sales, or insurance audits.

5. Communication Preferences & Opt-Out Rights

We send service reminders and seasonal monsoon advisories only when beneficial to your system's health. You may request deletion of your contact records or opt out of seasonal notifications at any time by emailing bookings@tankwise.example or calling our Peelamedu depot at +91 422 450 2820.

Thank you for trusting TankWise with the stewardship and health of your property's sanitation infrastructure.
