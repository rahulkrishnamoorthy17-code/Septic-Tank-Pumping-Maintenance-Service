To All Homeowners, Property Managers, and Commercial Facility Operators:
SUBJECT: Terms of Engagement, Mechanized Non-Entry Mandates, and Operational Scope

TankWise Septic Services operates on the foundational principle of professional, dignified, and mechanized infrastructure maintenance. Our services exist to safeguard public hygiene, protect property value, and eradicate manual sanitation risks.

By commissioning services through our website, telephone desk, or emergency dispatch unit, you agree to the operational conditions set forth in this formal service charter.

1. The Strict Non-Entry & Mechanized Work Mandate

Zero Manual Scavenging Policy: Under no circumstances will any TankWise personnel, technician, or contractor enter inside an underground septic tank, cesspool, or manhole. All liquid and sludge extraction is performed strictly through mechanized high-vacuum suction hoses. Diagnostic reviews utilize remote-controlled CCTV crawler cameras and optical probes.

2. Access Confirmation & Site Readiness

To ensure safe arrival and orderly execution of services:

  • The property owner must guarantee safe vehicular passage for vacuum tankers (standard truck width minimum 2.8 meters).
  • A direct, unobstructed pathway for flexible suction hoses from the truck parking position to the tank opening must be provided.
  • Children, domestic pets, and bystanders must remain strictly outside the designated 10-meter work safety perimeter during extraction.

3. Transparent Pricing & Onsite Scope Limits

Planning prices displayed on our website represent typical residential configurations. Final quotations are confirmed onsite upon assessing tank capacity, solid-to-liquid density, hose extension distances exceeding 30 meters, and cover accessibility.

A routine vacuum pumping visit removes accumulated sludge and scum layers to restore hydraulic working capacity. Pumping does not constitute a rebuild of collapsed drain fields, replacement of cracked brick masonry, or remediation of indoor water damage.

4. Authorized Municipal Transport & Environmental Compliance

All septage extracted by TankWise vacuum tankers is transported exclusively in sealed, leak-proof steel vessels to authorized Coimbatore Municipal Corporation wastewater treatment plants. TankWise strictly forbids open-field dumping, stormwater drain discharging, or unauthorized night disposals.

5. Payment Terms & Documentation

Payment is due upon completion of the service visit and presentation of the signed Digital Service Record. We accept UPI, bank transfer, and major credit cards. All clients receive an official itemized GST invoice and technician observation certificate.

We look forward to serving your property with uncompromised engineering precision and respect.
