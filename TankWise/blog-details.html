How often should a septic tank be pumped?

A septic tank does not empty itself. Solids accumulate continuously even when the liquid outlet is flowing freely. Here is the engineering reality of choosing the right pump-out interval.

TW
TankWise Technical Directorate
Coimbatore Field Operations
Key Engineering Takeaway

Pumping frequency is determined by active liquid volume, occupant count, and water habits rather than a static calendar interval. For most Coimbatore households, a 2-4 year cycle protects drain fields from catastrophic solids overflow.
